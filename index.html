Big feelings are easier with a friend.

VentPals gives every kid a bird companion. Together they name what the kid feels, practice calming skills, and grow. It all starts with an egg.

For kids ages 3 to 18. Parents, teachers, and counselors get their own grown-up area.

Every VentPal starts as an egg.

Checking in with a feeling, trying a breathing exercise, or writing a reflection earns points. Points hatch the egg, and keep helping your bird grow. Badges mark the big moments along the way.

Small things kids can do every day.

Each one takes a few minutes and gives kids a real skill they can use away from the screen.

  • Name the feeling

    Pick how you feel and your VentPal helps you find the words, then suggests a bird who understands.

  • Practice a calming skill

    Slow breathing, grounding, butterfly hugs, and mindful sips of water. See the skills

  • Explore the World

    Visit places like the Forest, Caves, and Stream, where each zone teaches something about feelings. Take a look

  • Visit a Nest

    Every bird has a Nest: a quiet place to learn what that bird teaches and write down a reflection.

Sixteen birds, sixteen ways of feeling.

Kids choose one bird when they join. That bird stays with them for good, so their growth is always theirs.
