The VentPals Flock

Meet the VentPals Flock

Every bird has its own personality and perspective. Children choose their own VentPal; other birds can offer temporary ideas without replacing that personal companion.

    Meet the Whole Flock 🦜

    Built for kids, trusted by grown-ups 💛

    Trusted grown-ups can be invited as Nest Guides. Children choose what they share; private reflections and AI conversations are not automatically shown to adults. The grown-up experience is being built alongside secure account connections.

    🔒 Privacy-first by design

    The development preview uses fictional details only. Secure accounts, verified adult connections, and production privacy safeguards will be completed before real child accounts are enabled.

    🪺 Child-led Nest Guides

    Grown-ups can offer support through trusted connections while respecting the child's choice about what to share.
