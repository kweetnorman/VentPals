What does a Nest Guide do?

A Nest Guide is an adult a child has chosen as someone they trust. You may offer encouragement and support through the permissions assigned to your connection.

  • You receive only the access explicitly granted to your role.
  • Private journal entries are not shared by default.
  • You cannot change the child's chosen name or VentPal.
  • You must respect the child's privacy, boundaries, and right to ask for help.

The child always gets the final say

After an adult accepts, the child sees the adult's verified display name and can confirm or decline. No connection becomes active until all required authorization and safeguarding checks are complete.
