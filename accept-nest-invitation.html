What does a Nest Guide do?

A Nest Guide is an adult a child has chosen as someone they trust. You may be invited to offer encouragement and support through the permissions assigned to your connection.

  • You receive only the access explicitly granted to your role.
  • You do not get access to private journal entries by default.
  • You cannot change the child's chosen name or VentPal.
  • You must respect the child's privacy, boundaries, and right to ask for help.

What happens after you accept?

The child is shown the verified adult's name and can confirm whether this is the person they asked to invite. No connection becomes active until all required authorization and safeguarding checks are complete.
