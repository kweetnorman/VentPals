Staying Safe 🔒

Before you begin, let's go over a few important rules about your privacy.

🙈
Your feelings belong to you.

This is a development preview, not a secure private account. Some choices and demo entries are saved in this browser. Use pretend details and do not enter anything you need to keep private.

🚫
Connections are not active yet.

This preview does not connect you with other people. Secure adult accounts and live Nest Guide connections will be introduced only after the safety features are ready.

👨‍👩‍👧
Trusted grown-ups can support you.

When secure adult connections are available, you will be able to ask for trusted grown-ups to join your Nest. Those connections are not active in this preview.

💜
You choose what to explore.

You can skip an activity, take a break, or choose another way to express yourself. You never have to explain a feeling before you are ready.

🆘
If something feels wrong, tell a trusted adult.

VentPals is here to help, but it's not a crisis service. If you ever feel unsafe, please talk to a trusted grown-up straight away.
