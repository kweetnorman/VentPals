About VentPals

A child-centered emotional-literacy experience where children can explore feelings, express themselves, and learn at their own pace with a bird companion they choose.

What VentPals Offers

VentPals helps children notice, name, and express feelings through reflection, creative activities, and playful learning. Each child chooses a VentPal bird companion that stays theirs through every feeling. VentPals is an educational experience, not therapy or a replacement for professional care.

The VentPals Flock

Children choose a VentPal because they like the bird—not because of how they feel. Their chosen companion stays with them through every feeling and every stage of growth.

Flockopedia introduces all sixteen bird companions, each with its own personality and story. Children can explore the flock in any order, with no pressure to choose a bird based on an emotion.

Safety First

VentPals is being developed with child safety, privacy, and choice in mind. The current app is a development preview: it uses local browser storage and does not provide secure accounts or live adult connections. Children and adults should use fictional details only.

Our Approach

We believe emotional learning happens through small, meaningful moments. VentPals offers reflection, creative expression, and opportunities to practice skills. A bird's growth is tied to learning activities—not to a child's feelings, behavior, check-ins, or emotional performance.
