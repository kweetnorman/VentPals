🔐 Two-key protection

Ordinary changes to a Main Guide connection require both Main Guides to approve. This prevents one adult from quietly removing the other.

Your own access

A Main Guide can always withdraw their own access without needing the other adult's permission.

🧡 Child safety comes first

Two-key protection must never trap a child in an unsafe connection. A private safeguarding route can pause access while a concern is reviewed, without requiring the child to confront the adult or get that adult's approval.

Safeguarding override

A restricted, documented safety hold bypasses ordinary two-key approval and triggers a protected review.
