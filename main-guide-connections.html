Secure QR preview

No invitation token exists

A real QR will be generated by the server for one selected child request. It will expire after 10 minutes and work once.

What the real QR will protect

  • One child and one pending request only
  • Random, opaque invitation token
  • 10-minute expiry and single use
  • Immediate cancellation and abuse controls
  • No child code, parent code, or personal details inside the QR

A QR scan starts a request. The adult must verify their own account, and the child must recognize and confirm the person before access can be granted.

The connection steps

1. Child asks

Child names the person they want in their Nest.

2. Guide invites

Main Guide generates a private, one-use QR.

3. Adult verifies

Invitee verifies identity and accepts their role.

4. Child confirms

Child confirms the person they requested.
