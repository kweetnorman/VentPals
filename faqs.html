🔒 Our Safety Model — Why VentPals Is Built This Way

Child preview

A demo username and PIN are stored in this browser. This is not a secure account. Use fictional details only.

Main Guides

The design allows two primary trusted adults. Secure adult accounts and live connections are not active in this preview.

Nest Guides

Children can ask to connect with trusted adults. Verification, approval, and live connections are planned—not active yet.

Child-led by design

The child’s choice comes first. Future connections will require identity checks, clear permissions, and the child’s confirmation.

VentPals is designed for children. Email accounts are often shared, monitored, or created by adults. Using email would give adults too much control over a child's emotional space.

Instead, children use:

  • A username they choose
  • A PIN they can remember
  • Parent approval for account activation

This keeps the child's identity private and safe.

Setup gives children a chance to choose their VentPal, create a display name, personalize their space, and learn about privacy before exploring their Nest. The current preview creates a local demo profile first; secure production accounts are not active yet.

In the planned experience, a verified Main Guide will help confirm the child’s account and support safe connections. Adult verification and account approval are not active in this development preview.

VentPals supports two primary Main Guides. They may be parents, guardians, or other trusted adults. Neither Main Guide can ordinarily remove or replace the other alone; both must approve an ordinary change. An adult can always leave their own connection, and a separate safeguarding path can pause an unsafe connection without waiting for the other adult.

A child can ask a Main Guide to invite a trusted teacher, counsellor, coach, or other adult. The invited adult must verify their identity and accept the role, and the child must confirm that this is the person they asked for. A QR scan alone never grants access. Secure adult connections are still being prepared and are not active in this preview.

The child starts the request. An authorized Main Guide helps prepare a private, short-lived invitation for that request. The adult verifies their own account, reviews the Nest Guide role, and accepts. The child then sees the adult's verified display name and can confirm or decline. The invitation process is currently a design preview; no real QR token or connection is created.

Ordinary changes to a Main Guide connection require both Main Guides to approve. That protection does not override a child's safety: a restricted safeguarding process can pause an unsafe connection without requiring the child to confront the adult or obtain that adult's approval. An adult can also withdraw their own access.

Yes. A child can privately ask for an additional Nest Guide to be removed and does not have to explain why. If a connection feels unsafe, the child can ask for help through a protected safeguarding route. The connection controls are still in development and are not active in this preview.

Because emotional safety is not about restricting children. It's about giving them language, autonomy, safe adults, tools, and control over their emotional world. VentPals protects children from emotional manipulation — not from connection.
