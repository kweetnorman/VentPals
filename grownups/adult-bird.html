What Is an Adult Companion Bird?

VentPals does not assign a bird based on a person’s feelings, behavior, or emotional style. If an adult companion experience is introduced, adults will choose whether to participate and how they want to engage with it. A symbolic companion may offer reflection prompts, but it will never define who you are or diagnose a pattern.

What Your Bird Will Help You Explore

  • • Your emotional triggers
  • • Your communication patterns
  • • Your inner child needs
  • • Your nervous system responses
  • • Your strengths and resilience
