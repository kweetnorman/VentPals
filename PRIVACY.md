# VentPals Development Preview Privacy Notice

**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

> **Important:** This notice describes the current development preview, not the final production service or a legal compliance determination. VentPals is still being built. Do not enter real child information, adult personal details, passwords, PINs used elsewhere, or shipping addresses. A production privacy policy must be reviewed and published before real child accounts are enabled.

## 1. Who we are

VentPals is being developed as a child-centered emotional literacy and self-discovery experience. It is designed to help children explore feelings, express themselves, learn skills, and connect with trusted adults on their own terms. VentPals is not therapy and does not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions.

**Contact:** [hello@ventpals.org](mailto:hello@ventpals.org)

## 2. What the current preview stores

The current child preview uses this browser's local storage for a fictional demo profile and app progress. Depending on the features explored, this can include:

- A special demo username and nickname.
- A four-digit demo PIN, stored using reversible Base64 encoding. **This is not secure password hashing. Do not reuse a real PIN.**
- The child's selected VentPal and avatar preferences.
- Demo growth points, badges, activity milestones, and keepsake entries.
- Reflections or other text the tester chooses to save in the preview.

The preview is limited to **one child demo profile per browser** because all app data has not yet been separated safely between multiple child profiles. Do not use it for multiple real children.

## 3. What is not active

The following features are intentionally deferred until the final authentication and security phase:

- Real child or adult account creation and sign-in.
- Verified adult-child connections and adult-managed child details.
- Parent/guardian PIN reset.
- Cloud account persistence and multi-child data separation.
- Physical keepsake fulfillment and shipping-address collection.

The child preview does not ask for a child's email address, legal name, or age. Grown-up registration forms are disabled. A connection code shown in the preview is not a verified relationship and must not be used to connect a real child.

## 4. Where preview information is stored

Demo profile information and saved app activity are stored in the browser's local storage on the device used for testing. Clearing browser data may erase it. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to inspect local storage or use the demo session. Local storage is not a secure account database.

The app loads some page assets and libraries from third-party content delivery and font services. The website host and those services may receive ordinary technical request information when a page or asset is loaded. This preview does not currently use Firebase to create or authenticate child accounts.

## 5. Child privacy principles being built into VentPals

The product is being designed around these principles:

- A child's permanent VentPal is selected by the child and is never assigned based on a feeling.
- Feelings are not scored, ranked, or used to reward or punish a child.
- A child's private reflections and AI conversations are not automatically shown to a grown-up.
- A child chooses what to share with a trusted Nest Guide.
- Growth rewards are intended to recognize exploration and learning milestones, not happiness, calmness, compliance, or daily check-in frequency.

These are product design goals. They should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every future feature is already implemented.

## 6. External services and links

VentPals may link to external services or load third-party page assets. Those services operate under their own privacy practices. The keepsake storefront is currently being refreshed; product checkout and physical fulfillment are not active in this preview.

## 7. Deleting preview information

You can clear the preview's local data by clearing this site's browser storage. This removes local demo information from that browser, but does not delete ordinary technical logs held by the website host or third-party asset providers.

Account deletion and verified data-deletion workflows will be designed and documented before production accounts are enabled.

## 8. Before production launch

Before real children or families use VentPals, the production service will need a completed privacy and security review, appropriate parental-consent and age-related flows, secure authentication, access controls, retention and deletion procedures, and a final privacy policy reviewed for applicable child-privacy laws.
