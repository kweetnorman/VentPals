How VentPals Works

A playful way for children to explore feelings, express themselves, and practice emotional-literacy skills.

VentPals combines emotional learning, creative activities, and bird companions. Children choose what to explore and move at their own pace.

1. Start With a Feeling

Kids begin by choosing a feeling that matches what’s happening inside — angry, sad, worried, invisible, excited, and more. No pressure. No judgment. Just honesty.

2. Meet Your VentPal

Your chosen VentPal stays with you through every feeling. The birds offer stories, prompts, and activities to help you find words for what is happening inside. Your feelings never choose or change your bird.

3. Grow Together

Kids can draw, write, or reflect with their VentPal. As they explore, learn, and practice new skills, their bird grows too — from egg to baby to nestling, feathered, and percher.

4. Join a Nest

Children can tell their Main Guides which trusted adults they would like to connect with. The child’s choice comes first. Secure adult accounts and live Nest Guide connections are not active in this development preview.

Keepsakes That Grow With Your Child

As a bird reaches a growth milestone, the preview can display a digital keepsake. Growth comes from exploring and practicing learning activities—not from feeling a certain way or sharing personal reflections.

Physical keepsakes and store checkout are not currently available. When the marketplace returns, it will offer optional products inspired by the VentPals birds.
